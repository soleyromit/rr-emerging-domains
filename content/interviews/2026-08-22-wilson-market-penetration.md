## Discipline prioritization (explicit)

> "MD/DO can be last, Pharmacy... Dentistry can be first."

Rationale given for Dentistry first: **no entrenched incumbent**, multiple competing
products already in the space — a genuine open field, unlike Pharmacy (ASHP
residency incumbent, see below) or MD/DO (highest complexity).

## Competitor-deck corrections flagged in this call

- **Project Concert** (concert.co) appears in Wilson's competitor deck tagged as
  serving the dental domain, but the company's own positioning is "providers, payers,
  and labs" — a different user base entirely (not universities). Flagged as a
  possible misclassification in the source deck, not confirmed corrected.
- **axiUm** (Henry Schein / Ascend Academic Cloud) — confirmed as a dental-specific,
  clinic-side product (patient/treatment management, scheduling, faculty-student
  management, competency assignment against patients) with **no documented
  university-enrollment/admissions side** — the read in this call was that axiUm
  competes on the clinic-operations side, and Exxat's placement/student side may be
  complementary rather than directly displacing it. A **mentorship** feature on axiUm
  was flagged as worth evaluating — not currently a known Exxat capability.
- **ExamSoft** — confirmed to have exclusive features Exxat lacks; some universities
  run both products side by side rather than choosing one — a coexistence pattern,
  not pure head-to-head competition.

## ASHP / pharmacy residency (separate from PharmD/APPE)

Pharmacy residency (post-graduate, hospital-department-based, matched via ASHP,
PGY-1 competency areas parallel to ACGME milestones) has **a named incumbent already
listed on the ASHP website** that programs are effectively directed to use. Verbatim
framing: "that's a long-term strategy, that will have a policy play... you'll have to
figure out how do you get into that website to be recommended." Distinct from
continuing-education, which runs through separate third-party CE websites feeding
directly into required CE hours.

## Pharmacy standards timing — a real near-term opening

> "The new standards in Pharmacy... got published in 2025... after 10 years... all
> the schools must be upgrading to those standards already."

Framed as a concrete sales window: "this other new standards Exxat already provides
— Exxat already has services here, and if there are any gaps, what are those gaps."
The suggested pitch adds cross-domain breadth: "you can get one software that can be
compliant, that can do everything, as well as your other domains."

## Existing warm relationships (needs sales/account-manager confirmation, not yet verified here)

University of North Carolina Chapel Hill named as a top Pharmacy school where Exxat

## Axiom-style "connector" integration pattern (a general strategic idea, not yet evaluated)

Proposed pattern for point-solution competitors with a narrow but sticky wedge (the
axiUm/Canvas analogy): rather than displacing the whole product, connect via API so
Exxat processes/reports on data the other system owns for one specific job (worked
example: exam results already live in Canvas; Exxat ties that data to its own
course/faculty-performance reporting via API rather than building exam delivery
itself). Explicitly named as the model for how Exxat's own not-yet-built Exam
Management pillar might eventually relate to Canvas/ExamSoft rather than competing
head-on.
