# Go-to-market strategy and pharmacy research planning

## The seven research inputs, and what they feed

The session named the shape of the go-to-market research effort itself: seven kinds of
input that a domain gets dissected with — **competitive analysis, market sizing,
program-level research, domain-expert research, conference research, interviews, and
consultants**. They feed product development first, and sales second.

## The template already exists

Vishaka's own pharmacy document was named explicitly as **the template to replicate** for
every future domain — not a one-off artifact for pharmacy, but the shape each of the
remaining emerging domains should be worked up into.

## The step-one question

The question this whole effort exists to answer, stated plainly: **what are the gaps for
us to launch Exxat Prism in pharmacy** — starting from what Exxat already has for
nursing, PT and OT, what transfers as-is, and what is a real gap. Account manager
**Ashish** was named as the next person to talk to; he is working with one university's
pharmacy implementation.

## Scope discipline

An explicit decision to hold scope: **pharmacy only for now** (pharmacy technician
possibly alongside it). Not alumni engagement, not career readiness, not other adjacent
areas.

## Collateral is someone else's job

Whitepapers, podcasts, webinars, videos, use cases and sandbox pitch decks are owned by
**marketing (Isha, Virangi)** and **sales (Sam)** — not by this research effort. This repo
tracks that collateral and points at it; it does not produce it. Keeping that line clear
is part of the scope discipline above.

## On method

A closing note on how the work gets done, and a deliberate pushback against shortcutting
it: *"you can't just use AI for everything… we need to understand it ourselves."* AI-only
synthesis is not a substitute for the seven inputs — the research has to be done, and
understood, by the people doing it.
