## A. Positioning statement & key differentiators

**Positioning statement:** Prism wins on placement-gating compliance and modern mobile UX — not on feature-for-feature accreditation-reporting parity, which the incumbents currently win. Lead with the two strongest fits in the entire four-domain corpus, not a broad capability claim.

**Why MD programs would consider switching:**

1. **No MD incumbent publicly documents a dedicated compliance module.** one45, Elentra, and Leo show no public evidence of immunization/background-check/certification gating as a standalone pillar — Prism's shipped Compliance Management is a real, provable gap.
2. **Central clerkship-completion monitoring is Prism's strongest fit anywhere in this research.** LCME 8.6 is rated the single strongest standard-to-pillar match across all four domains studied.
3. **The incumbent field is in real M&A churn.** Leo customers are being told by their own post-merger parent (Elentra) that a migration is coming; New Innovations faces roadmap uncertainty post-QGenda acquisition (Jul 2025). A program already being told a change is coming will evaluate alternatives it would otherwise never open.

*(`personas/discipline-medicine.yaml`, `accreditation/lcme.yaml`)*

**Be honest about where we're behind:** six of seven MD incumbents already ship exams, surveys, and standards-mapped accreditation reporting today — capabilities on Prism's 2027 roadmap. Don't contest this; it isn't the pitch.

## C. Objection handling

**"[MedHub/one45/Elentra] already does exams and accreditation reporting — why would we add Prism?"**
Don't compete there — concede it. Lead with what none of them ship: a dedicated, discrete compliance-gating module (immunizations, background checks, certifications) at placement start, plus central clerkship-completion monitoring rated the strongest fit in this entire research effort.

**"We're happy with our current system."**
Ask what the mobile data-entry experience is like for residents/students in the field — MedHub's mobile app and one45's interface both draw documented complaints. That's a real, provable wedge, not a generic UX claim.

**"We're on Leo/New Innovations and not looking to move."**
Leo's own post-merger parent (Elentra) is publicly marketing migration paths off Leo; New Innovations faces roadmap uncertainty after its July 2025 acquisition by QGenda. This program may be evaluating alternatives sooner than it expects, on a timeline it didn't choose.

**"What does this cost?"**
No public pricing exists across any of the seven MD-relevant competitors — all confirmed quote-based/non-public. Don't improvise a number.

*(`competitors/medhub.yaml`, `competitors/one45.yaml`, `competitors/leo-davinci.yaml`, `competitors/new-innovations.yaml`)*

## D. Pricing & talking points

**Pricing:** Not defined for Medicine in this repo — and uniquely among the four domains, *no* competitor here has any public price signal at all (unlike Pharmacy/DO, where at least one vendor does).

**ROI framing (qualitative — no sourced dollar figures):**
- Prove every required clerkship was completed, centrally, before it becomes a self-study gap (LCME 8.6).
- Auto-trigger midpoint feedback and grading off placement dates instead of chasing faculty by email (LCME 9.7/9.8).
- Bring away rotations — over half of fourth-years, $2,500–$10,000+ typical cost — into the same system as home clerkships instead of leaving them in VSLO and email.
