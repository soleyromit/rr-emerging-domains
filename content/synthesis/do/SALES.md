## A. Positioning statement & key differentiators

**Positioning statement:** Prism is the system of record for clinical/experiential education, and COCA's 2026 standards ask for exactly the evidence our placement engine already produces — at the moment four of the plausible DO incumbents are in M&A churn.

**Why DO programs would consider switching:**

1. **Vendor discontinuity many COMs didn't choose.** New Innovations → QGenda (Jul 2025), MedHub/E\*Value → Ascend Learning, Leo/DaVinci folded into Elentra under Achieve Partners — with Elentra now publicly inviting Leo's own customers to migrate off it. A program being told to move anyway evaluates the field rather than moving where it's pushed.
2. **The two strongest curriculum players ship no compliance module.** Elentra and Leo lead on curriculum/accreditation depth but neither publicly documents a compliance-tracking module — immunization, background-check, and affiliation-agreement tracking likely lives in spreadsheets regardless of which suite the COM owns.
3. **The COCA 2026 clock is forcing a evidence rebuild regardless of vendor.** 6.9/6.10's system-generated proof requirements raise the bar on exactly the data the distributive clinical model makes hardest to produce.

## C. Objection handling

**"We're on [Elentra/Leo] and love the curriculum tools — why switch?"**
Don't contest curriculum depth — Elentra and Leo are genuinely strong there (AAMC CI vendor status, >40% CACMS DCI coverage). Ask instead: where does immunization, background-check, and affiliation-agreement tracking actually live today? Neither vendor publicly ships a compliance module — that's very likely a spreadsheet, not a system.

**"We're already moving to [Elentra] because Leo/New Innovations is being sunset — why not just go there?"**
That's exactly the forced-migration window worth using to evaluate the whole field, not just the vendor doing the courting. A migration is happening either way; make it a real comparison, not a default.

**"Does Prism actually cover COCA-specific requirements, or is this generic?"**
Yes to the operational specifics: 3-year rolling site-capacity averages, per-student pre-4th-year supervision checklists, OMM/OPP competency mapping. Be honest about the real gaps — cross-site statistical comparability (6.11) and the annual/mid-cycle report aggregation (11.9) are not shipped yet; New Innovations already ships accreditation-specific tooling (APE, NAS, CLER) Prism doesn't plan until Q3 2027.

**"What does this cost?"**
No public pricing exists across any of the five DO-relevant competitors except eMedley's Capterra-listed $5,000/year flat starting price. Don't improvise a number.
