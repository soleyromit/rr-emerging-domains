## Read this before anything else

**Dentistry is deferred, and the reason is structural, not a ranking artifact.** Per `scorecard/where-to-play.yaml`, Dentistry scores lowest of the four domains (2.75 weighted) — the clinical model itself (a longitudinal in-house patient panel with per-procedure chairside sign-off) doesn't match Prism's rotation-based placement engine, and the incumbent (axiUm, ~90% penetration) is a combined patient EHR + billing + gradebook system with near-absolute retention, not a displaceable point solution. The domain's own persona file is explicit: *this archetype does not rip out axiUm* — any viable play here is additive (a new school's full stack, or a growing program's extramural-placement gap), not a switch pitch. See PRODUCT.md and SALES.md for what that changes.
