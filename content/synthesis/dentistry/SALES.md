## A. Positioning statement & key differentiators — and one hard rule

**The rule first:** this is not a displacement pitch. axiUm is the combined patient EHR + billing/revenue-cycle + student gradebook for the teaching clinic — one database, HIPAA-regulated. The domain's own research is explicit: *"This archetype does not rip out axiUm."* Any pitch built around replacing it will fail and burns credibility. *(`personas/discipline-dentistry.yaml`, "switching_trigger")*

**Positioning statement:** Prism is the system that covers what a dental school's clinic-EHR was never built for — placing students at outside sites when in-house patient volume can't meet CODA's quota, and keeping the compliance/agreement trail that goes with it.

**Two real openings, not three:**

1. **A brand-new school** buying its whole stack at once — the only moment axiUm-vs-alternative is a genuinely open decision (no legacy database to migrate).
2. **A growing program's first extramural expansion**, forced by patient-volume shortfall — a budgeted *adjacent* purchase, not a switch.

**The disqualifier to know before you're in the room:** any pitch requiring CDT-level (tooth/surface/procedure-code) logging or replacing chairside faculty sign-off is competing with axiUm's core, not its gaps. Don't go there.

## C. Objection handling

**"We already run axiUm — why would we need anything else?"**
You don't need to switch. axiUm covers the in-house clinic well; the gap is what happens when patient volume forces students to community-based extramural sites — axiUm has no public external-placement engine or student compliance tracker for that. This conversation only exists if that pressure is real for them.

**"CORE ELMS also serves dental — why not them?"**
CORE's dental page makes no CODA-specific claim, unlike its explicit ACPE claim on the pharmacy page — a fair, provable comparison point if the conversation gets there.

**"We're a new/newly-accredited school — do we even need axiUm?"**
This is the one conversation where the whole stack is genuinely open. Lead with the full picture, not just the placement gap.

**"Does Prism handle CDT coding / chairside sign-off?"**
Be straight: no, and that's not the pitch. Prism's opening is the extramural-placement and compliance layer around the clinic, not the clinic system itself.
